Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting PNG to JPG?

Since the JPG format does not support transparency, transparent areas are cleanly filled with a crisp white background.

Will converting to JPG reduce my file size?

Yes! For photographs and complex images, converting PNG to JPG typically reduces file size by 50% to 75%.

Can I convert multiple PNG files at once?

Yes, batch format conversion is supported in the Studio workspace.
