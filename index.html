❓ Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using PixKit for free image and PDF processing.

How do I merge PDF files for free without uploading them to a third-party server?
Click on Merge PDF, drop all your PDF documents into the workspace, drag the files to order them, and click Merge & Download. Because PixKit runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly, none of your documents are ever transmitted to any external server.
How do I convert PDF pages into high-resolution JPG or PNG images?
Open the PDF to Images tool, select your PDF file, choose your preferred format (JPG, PNG, or WEBP) and DPI resolution (72 DPI for web, up to 300 DPI for print), and download pages individually or as a complete ZIP package.
How does in-browser image compression work without losing quality?
PixKit analyzes your image matrix locally and optimizes high-frequency pixel data without altering visible color fidelity. You can adjust the visual quality slider with a real-time side-by-side preview before exporting.
Can I rotate or delete specific pages from a PDF document?
Yes! Use Rotate PDF to turn portrait or landscape pages 90° or 180° clockwise with lossless rotation, or use Delete PDF Pages to click on unwanted pages and export a clean PDF with those pages removed.
Is PixKit completely free to use?
Yes, PixKit is 100% free with no subscriptions, watermarks, or artificial file limits. You can install it on your Mac, Windows, or Android device as an offline Progressive Web App (PWA).
