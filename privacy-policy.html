Privacy Policy.
Your data never leaves your device.

PixKit Studio is engineered with an uncompromising privacy-first principle: your personal images, sensitive PDF files, and creative edits are processed 100% locally in your device's memory. We do not operate file upload servers, we do not inspect your media, and we never collect, track, or monetize your data.

Last Updated: November 12, 2026 Zero-Server Verified GDPR & COPPA Compliant

Zero Cloud Uploads

Every edit, resize, conversion, and PDF operation runs strictly inside your web browser's RAM via WebAssembly and Canvas APIs.

No Accounts or Sign-Ups

Use every single tool instantly without creating an account, sharing your email, or linking social media profiles.

Zero Ads & Trackers

No third-party ad networks, no tracking beacons, no fingerprinting scripts, and zero cross-site behavioral profiling.

Works Offline (PWA)

Once cached, PixKit runs completely offline without any internet connection required. True local-first software.

PixKit Studio ("we", "our", or "the platform") is dedicated to providing creative image and document utility tools that protect individual privacy rights. This Privacy Policy details our operational practices, data boundaries, and local-first technological architecture across our web platform (pixkit.site) and Android mobile applications.
01

Zero Personal Data Collection

PixKit Studio is designed from the ground up as a zero-knowledge, local utility. We do not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell any of the following:

  • Personal Identity: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or identification numbers.
  • Device & Biometric Data: Hardware serial numbers, IMEI, MAC addresses, facial recognition geometry, or biometrics.
  • Network Identifiers: We do not log, retain, or link IP addresses to your creative projects.
Key Takeaway: You can use PixKit completely anonymously. We don't know who you are, what files you open, or what you create.
02

Local On-Device File Processing (How It Works)

Unlike traditional online converters that upload your files to remote cloud servers to process them, PixKit executes all processing directly inside your web browser or device memory:

  • Image Operations: Resizing, cropping, tone adjustment, filters, drawing, format conversions (JPG, PNG, WebP, SVG, HEIC), and compression run entirely through HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, and browser Web Workers.
  • PDF Suite Operations: Merging, splitting, rotating, compressing, and page deletions are handled through compiled client-side WebAssembly libraries executing purely in your local RAM.
  • Immediate Memory Disposal: When you close or refresh your browser tab, memory used by your images is immediately cleared and recycled by your operating system.
03

Device Storage Access & Permissions

PixKit requests access to your file system or photo library solely through standard browser file pickers (`<input type="file">` or the File System Access API) when you deliberately choose to edit a file:

  • We only read the specific files you select for editing.
  • We do not scan, index, inspect, or background-read your device storage or photo albums.
  • Exported files are saved back to your device via standard browser download triggers.
04

No Third-Party Advertising or Profiling

PixKit Studio is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense, Meta Audience Network, or programmatic display SDKs), behavioral retargeting pixels, or data brokers. Your activity is never sold or shared with any advertiser or commercial entity.

05

Cookies and Local Storage

PixKit does not use tracking cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. We only utilize your browser's standard LocalStorage for the following purely functional, on-device preferences:

  • pixkit_theme: Saves your preferred visual theme (`dark` or `light`) so it remains comfortable on subsequent visits.
  • Recent Works (Studio): Saves your active canvas state locally on your machine so you don't lose work if you accidentally refresh. This data never leaves your device.
  • Service Worker Cache: Allows PixKit's HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files to load instantly and function offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA).
06

Third-Party Services & Voluntary Support

If you choose to voluntarily support PixKit through our Support Page:

  • Payments are handled directly on secure external payment gateways (PayPal or official UPI apps).
  • PixKit never collects, sees, or stores your credit card numbers, CVVs, bank account numbers, or payment credentials.
  • If you voluntarily choose to appear on the Supporter Leaderboard, only your chosen public display name and message are recorded.
07

Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR)

Because PixKit Studio collects zero personal identifiable information from any user of any age, our services are fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). PixKit is safe for students, educators, children, and professional creators alike.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements in technology or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. Our core commitment—zero server file uploads and 100% client-side privacy—will never change.
