Privacy Policy for PixKit Studio

Last updated: October 31, 2026

PixKit Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and data processing.

1. Zero Data Collection

PixKit Studio is designed from the ground up as a private, local-first utility. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, or biometric data.

2. Local On-Device File Processing

All photo editing, filtering, format conversions, and PDF manipulations happen entirely inside your device's local memory. None of your photos, images, or documents are ever uploaded to remote servers or cloud databases.

3. Device Storage Access

The app requests access to your photos or file storage solely for the purpose of opening files you deliberately choose to edit, and saving your finished export files back to your device. We do not access, scan, or index files in your storage without your direct interaction.

4. No Third-Party Trackers or Advertising

PixKit Studio does not contain any third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), advertising networks, or user profiling cookies. The app operates with zero ad tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

Because PixKit Studio collects zero personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR regulations and safe for users of all ages.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the PixKit development team directly at support.pixkitstudio@gmail.com.
