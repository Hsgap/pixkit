Where Your Contribution Goes

We believe in total transparency. Every contribution directly funds the following pillars:

Edge CDN & High-Speed Distribution

Finances ultra-fast edge caching across global Fastly & Cloudflare nodes so tools load instantly in milliseconds anywhere in the world.

Privacy-First & Clean Workflow

Most "free" converters sell your files or harvest personal data. PixKit pledges to keep your documents strictly on your device and protect everyday essentials from unfair paywalls.

Continuous WebAssembly Tool Engineering

Funds ongoing performance research to write faster client-side algorithms for RAW image processing, PDF manipulation, and privacy redaction.

Offline Mobile & Desktop PWA Apps

Supports development of offline Android APK and Progressive Web App builds so you can edit photos and PDFs on airplanes or without internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about supporting our independent development.

Is PixKit really 100% client-side with zero server storage?
Yes. All processing runs directly in your browser. Your files never touch an external server or cloud storage.
What payment methods are supported?
We support international contributions via PayPal and global debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex). For Indian supporters, we integrate domestic Razorpay checkout supporting Indian UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), RuPay cards, and 50+ banks via Netbanking in INR (₹) with zero international fees.
Will PixKit ever put tools behind a paid subscription or paywall?
Our core tools will always remain free with no mandatory accounts or watermarks. Optional contributions and future pro features help support continuous development.
Can I request a new tool or feature?
Absolutely! Send your suggestions or feature ideas anytime to support.pixkitstudio@gmail.com.
